Write a 1 to 2 page review for the design in the REQUIREMENTS.md file.

You should have access to the REQUIREMENTS.md file in the workspace. Let me

I'm looking for the kind of feedback that domain experts
like a senior product manager or a senior engineer might offer. Don't
focus on the details of the design: I'm not looking for editorial/wording nitpicks.

Here are some topics to consider:
* Would vcrd be a valuable tool for people who are working in the area of verifiable credentials? Are there already tools that provide the functionality proposed for vcrd-core and/or vcrd-cli?
* What might be the profile(s) of people who would find vcrd useful?
* What would motivate people in the verifiable credentials community to consider contributing to the open source vcrd code base?
* Are there important security risks that the design does not cover?
* Are there ethical issues that the design does not address?
* Are there legal issues that the design does not address?
* Are there major areas of the design (functionality, language, structure, approach) that should be reconsidered?
